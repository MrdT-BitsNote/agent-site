Capability

Four pillars. It cuts options, not information.

No wall of tips. Given a fixed budget, day count and energy level, it removes half the options and orders the rest so every line is executable.

Domestic transport comparison

Ring distance first, then mode: within 30 km, 30–150 km suburban, beyond 150 km intercity. High-speed rail, flight, self-drive, coach, carpool and charter compared in one table, with the walk from the arrival station.

Room splitting and budget

Room count and bed type come from relationship times gender, not headcount divided by two. Nights, district and the basis for each budget line come out together, so you can reconcile before booking.

Kernel

If it cannot be verified, it says so. That is a constraint, not a style choice.

Facts sit in three tiers, each with exactly one handling rule. Model memory may not be used to fill in prices, visa status or opening hours.

Since v0.2.1 the decision logic itself has to survive counter-examples. Even at a clean A/100 static score, nine places still read “October 3” as three days, mistook “help me write a report” for a motorcycle trip, and compared the last bus against a last ferry.
All nine are fixed and locked behind 44 counter-example assertions — each one records the original wording that failed, then asserts it can no longer recur.

stable

Stable facts

Geography, culture, institutions. Taken from the local base, sourced and re-checkable.

volatile

Volatile items must be verified

Fares, schedules, opening hours, quotas, visa policy, road conditions. Verified with a date stamp; anything stale is marked “re-check before departure”.

unknown

Grey light, no conclusion

Not found, or the source is unclear. It only says where to look and what to check — no room for guesswork.

Quick Start

Three steps to your first booklet

No dependencies to install; the scripts use the Python standard library only. If the host has no network access it degrades and says so.

1Install

Match the layout in the matrix above, then upload the pack to the right entry. Expert packs go to the expert entry, skill packs to the skill entry — do not mix them. Three marketplaces are already live.

2Describe the trip

One sentence is enough: three days in Zhangjiajie from Foshan, two people, sunrise hike, budget 3000. Missing parameters are collected in two short rounds, not one long form.

3Get the booklet

You get a compact A5 card-style HTML booklet with 13 sections: requirement card, transport comparison, lodging, day-by-day routing, budget breakdown, gate lights and a re-check list.
