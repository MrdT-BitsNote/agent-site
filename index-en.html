03 · PRE-TRIP CHECK

Pre-trip check: can this trip actually happen?

Riding a moto, driving an RV, or going abroad follows completely different rules than driving a car — it checks "can this go, is it legal, any traps" item by item before you leave, and tells you in plain terms.

ConstraintCarMotorcycle / RV / outbound handling
LicenseAny valid licenseClass must match (riding a moto on a C1 counts as unlicensed); RV ≥6m or ≥4,500kg needs B/A license, trailer needs C6
Highway / heightJust goHighway varies by province: 17 of 31 provinces ban motorcycles on highways — those just warn you to take national roads (+40–80% time); only deliberately breaking the ban gets stopped; RV height checked against measured height
City limitsDetour possibleVaries by city: 45 cities ban or restrict motorcycles — entering may get the bike impounded
Range / supply600–800km, dense stations200–400km, mountain gaps up to 150km; refuel action sequences; RV supply radius computed at ~85L water/person/day
Outbound admission—Passport / visa / vaccine / insurance four documents checked first — fail and it stops before any itinerary; unlisted countries are marked "unknown"
Branch🚫 Can't go⚠️ Go, but handle first❓ Unknown — won't guess
🏍 MotorcycleLicense mismatch still riding; highway planned in a banning provinceHighway-banned province; moto-banned city; tight rangeLocal enforcement practice unverified
🚐 RVLicense vs. vehicle legal requirement mismatch still drivingHeight limits uncertain; sparse supply; camp compliance pendingOvernight camp eligibility not phone-confirmed
🛂 OutboundAny of visa / passport / vaccine / insurance failsDocuments in process; peak-season queuesCountries with unlisted policy
Two disciplines locked by 465 assertions: "highway banned" ≠ "can't go" — national roads always work; re-verify policy 1–3 days before departure, one phone call beats one impoundment. Route library discipline: only stable facts (existence, direction, terrain, season) — never gas station coordinates or live road conditions; those must be checked on the day.

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

QIt says the trip can't happen — what now?
"Can't happen" means an illegal or hard condition is unresolved — the booklet then renders a stop page instead of an itinerary, listing only what must be resolved first and where to verify. Fix each item and re-run with adjusted parameters. "Handle first" is a to-do list; "unknown" means unverified (check yourself — the system won't guess). Meanings: see "03 Pre-trip check".
QAre fares, tickets and weather accurate?
Two hard rules: what can't be found is never invented — only query paths and booking timing are given; only verified data is labeled — weather comes from Open-Meteo with verification dates, station codes are cached. Full scope: "04 Data & trust".
QCan it book tickets or hotels? Can I print a PDF?
Booking no — and it doesn't pretend to: transactions need payment permissions and live inventory. PDF yes: single-file HTML, Ctrl+P saves directly with navigation auto-hidden and margins compressed. Changing requirements doesn't mean re-explaining: only the dimension you mention re-runs.
QCan motorcycles use the highway? How does it decide?
It ships 31 provinces of highway access rules (13 allow / 17 ban / 1 conditional) and 45 cities with moto bans, each with an evidence level. A highway ban never stops you — it only tells you to take national roads (+40–80% time); a red light appears only if you insist on the highway. Unverified cities are always grey — never a silent green.
QSkill or Agent app — which should I choose?
Same capability core, different entry and ownership: a Skill installs into an existing Agent in a minute but lives in someone else's app; an Agent app runs on your own server with your own domain, data staying local, zero capability trimming. See "05 Install into your Agent (two forms)".
